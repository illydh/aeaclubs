# AEA Connect Hub

i am basically making an internal website for employees at my workplace to be able to view the different orgs available to join. we really only need a main/landing page, clubs, documents and photos page. On that main page we can we could have a short welcome and a little bit about AEA. On that page we could have a calendar where upcoming events could be posted. And we could have a link for folks to contact us with suggestions or comments contactaea@aero.org

the landing page could include the officers and directors. also it could have a calendar shown where we can have our clubs add events that they may have coming up. I think it would be nice to have a nice short write up welcoming folks to the club page and we’re here to make their time at the company that much better… something friendly and inviting so they want to look further. We could also have a link on that page for people to contact us. It could state something like we welcome your thoughts, suggestions and questions. Reach out to us at contactaea@aero.org. Officers/Directors page combined if we don’t want them on the main page
We will want the clubs page listing the various clubs. we want to be interactive so that people will be able to click on links to connect.
A documents page for bylaws and whatnot
And maybe a page with photos of activities.
we want it to be simple but something appealing and easy to use and at least from this decade
the logo (attached image) should be included
